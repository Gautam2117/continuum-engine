## What the research does not establish

Open source tooling does not automatically provide a source of uniquely valuable stories, legally reusable media, correctly timed 3D environments or guaranteed virality. Many YouTube growth claims come from selective case studies. Use an original channel-specific visual grammar, benchmark finished quality against the source material, and compare like-for-like duration and audience before interpreting audience metrics.

Official platform constraints:

- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 (inauthentic or repetitive monetization restrictions)
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851 (YPP thresholds, independent per channel)
- https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/determine_quota_cost (per-project upload request limits; inspect actual quotas)
- https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/revision_history (2 November 2026 upload policy revision)
- https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/channel_reports (authorized retention reports)

Do not obtain deceptive engagement, bypass API limits, copy protected character art, use leaked credentials, or use provider free tiers in violation of their terms. No monetary commitment is authorized by this research.
